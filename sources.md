## Source discipline

- Synthetic case details are labelled as fictional; do not attribute them to named clients or vendors.
- A public HMRC charter is not evidence of an HMRC project or endorsement.
- “Purview supports” is constrained to Microsoft Learn documentation; connector, license and exact workflow are not assumed.
- Regulatory obligations must be determined by the responsible organisation based on actual processing, current law, regulator material and specialist advice.
